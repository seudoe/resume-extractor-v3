  - The base is GLiNER2.5, which wraps DeBERTa-v3-base (a 12-layer encoder,

    descriptions ("company: employer name"), and it points at the spans that
    match.

    parameters, in a 7 MB file.
  - It was trained on 7,965 entry blocks, from synthetic resumes (exact labels)
    and LiveCareer resumes (labels from their HTML tags). No LLM was involved.
  - It lifted job-title F1 from 71 to 97 and company from 83 to 94 on template
    layouts it never saw.

  2. Retraining on resume-data: docs/RETRAINING.md has the verdict, the evidence
  and the exact commands.

  Are those PDFs and JSONs right to train on? Mostly no. Measured on the files:
  - Layout is not diverse: all 150 sampled PDFs use the same font and 82% are
    single-column. Another 2,400 of them won't teach the model new layouts,
    which is where its real weakness is.
  - The content is useful: 24 professions help with non-tech job titles and
    schools, since my synthetic data is mostly IT roles.

    company or the literal "Company Name", because the source resumes are
    anonymised. Only 289 of the 2,484 JSONs exist so far; your generation is
    still running.

    aligned at 99.9%, and 92.5% of the HTML titles also appear in the JSONs.
